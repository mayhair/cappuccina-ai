# Ta Ta Ta Sahur

Aliases: The Teapot Ghost, Il Bollitore
Blurb: A somber copper kettle.

Ta Ta Ta Sahur is an Indonesian "brainrot" character. He is a copper teapot (sometimes drawn as a kettle) with human legs and an arm sprouting from his handle, wide blue eyes, and a worried face, set in a night-city scene. He's often shown crying, with steam coming out of him. Like Tung Tung Tung Sahur, he's linked to the Indonesian dawn-call idea — he "hunts" people who ignore the sahur wake-up call. He even goes by nicknames like "The Teapot Ghost" or "Il Bollitore" (Italian for "the kettle"). He's part of a whole family of Sahur characters — To To To Sahur, Ti Ti Ti Sahur, Te Te Te Sahur, and so on.
