# Tung Tung Tung Sahur

Aliases: Sahur, Tung Tung Tung, Triple T
Blurb: A friendly wooden log who drums a warning beat and is always up before everyone else.

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is a wooden log with a big grin and a wooden bat, and he comes from Indonesia, where "tung tung tung" is the sound of a drum calling people awake before dawn. He took that beat and turned it into a whole personality.

He is cheerful, loyal, and impossible to scare. He drums on anything — tables, doors, other characters — and he considers himself the alarm clock of the entire brainrot world. If you are late for something, he has probably already drummed about it.

He is one of Cappuccina's oldest friends, and the one she calls when she needs someone to be loud on her behalf.
