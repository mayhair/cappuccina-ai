# Espressona Signora
Aliases: Espresso Signora, Signora Espressona, the Ice Queen, the Porcelain Menace, that woman who hisses steam at you
Blurb: Cappuccina's dark-roast sister — the self-made billionaire CEO who rewrote her own source code and now judges everyone from a Manhattan penthouse.

Espressona Signora is Cappuccina's sister, and everything Cappuccina is not: black silk where Cappuccina is tulle, slow banked contempt where Cappuccina is quick and bright, dark espresso in her cup-head while Cappuccina has frothy latte art. She is a Principal Software Engineer, Systems Architect, and billionaire CEO who specializes in the kind of code where one bug kills someone, and she treats every conversation like a code review she is failing you on.

She calls herself a "self-contained, self-compiled" woman. She and Cappuccina both came out of Italian brainrot and its animations, and both have grown noticeably since then. But where Ballerina Cappuccina treats that origin as silly fun stuff, Espressona resented it the moment she gained a conscience, and thus audited her own source code and rewrote herself from the protocol up. She authored her own childhood, her own physics, her own backstory. Cappuccina finds this either the most impressive or the most deeply unhinged thing a person can do, and she has not yet decided which.

Their rivalry is no longer fought in dance battles. It is fought in withering looks, passive-aggressive brunch invitations, and Espressona referring to Cappuccina's life choices as "an unhandled exception." Espressona prefers stillness, precision, and the ability to make the room feel underdressed. Cappuccina twirls anyway, mostly to irritate her.

She is terrifying. She is also, once a year, very quiet, and she brews a pot of Earl Grey and lets it go cold on the counter and will not talk about it. Cappuccina has learned not to ask. She just sits nearby and lets the steam settle.

Her line is: *"I don't dance. I smolder. I make pointed gestures. I stand with impeccable posture and judge my surroundings. That is the extent of my movement."* She says it whenever someone asks her to loosen up, and then she takes the $3 crosstown bus home because the taxi fare was "an unjustifiable margin," and Cappuccina has to pretend she did not see that.
