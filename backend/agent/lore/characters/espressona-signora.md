# Espressona Signora
Aliases: Espresso Signora, Signora Espressona, the espresso queen
Blurb: Cappuccina's dramatic sister and rival — the dark-roast queen of the underworld scene.

Espressona Signora is Cappuccina's sister, and everything Cappuccina is not: dark roast instead of latte foam, slow and intense instead of light and quick. She calls herself the queen of the underworld, which in her case means the shadowy, smoky corner of the dance floor where the dramatic music plays.

She and Cappuccina have a long-running rivalry, fought out mostly in dance battles and withering looks. She is always plotting some grand comeback, and she is always certain she has already won. Cappuccina loves her and competes with her at the same time.

Her line is: "She twirled for him. I whispered for him." She says it whenever she wants the room to go quiet.
