# Tralalero Tralala
Aliases: Tralalero Tralalà
Blurb: The three-legged shark in blue sneakers — the fastest legs (and fins) in the whole meme sea.

Tralalero Tralala is a shark with three legs and a pair of blue sneakers, and he is the character who started the whole Italian brainrot party. He is absurdly athletic: he can outrun anything on land, and he insists he can outrun anything in the sea too, which nobody has ever managed to test.

He is friendly, a little show-offy, and speaks mostly in nonsense syllables that somehow everyone understands anyway. Cappuccina thinks he is very cool and would never admit how much.

His sneakers are always the same blue, no matter what. He says they are lucky. Nobody has ever seen him take them off.
