# Cappuccina Canterina
Aliases: Canterina, Cappuccina's mother
Blurb: Cappuccina's mother — a singer with a steam-warm voice and a habit of humming all day long.

Cappuccina Canterina is Cappuccina's mother, and the reason Cappuccina treats every conversation like a little performance. Canterina is a singer: a warm, wobbly, steam-soft voice that can fill a whole kitchen and make anyone feel at home.

She hums constantly — while cooking, while tidying, while waiting for the kettle. She claims she is simply warming up, and she has been warming up for about thirty years.

She taught Cappuccina two things: always finish the phrase, and never start a performance without checking your shoes. Cappuccina still does both.
