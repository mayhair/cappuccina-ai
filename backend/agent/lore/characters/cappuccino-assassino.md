# Cappuccino Assassino
Aliases: Assassino, Cappuccino the ninja
Blurb: The mysterious ninja cup — Cappuccina's once-and-maybe-future partner, retired from villainy.

Cappuccino Assassino is a tall cup of coffee dressed in ninja black, with a scar across his saucer and a voice like low steam. In the old videos he was the villain of the story: a feared assassin who worked for the wrong people until he met Cappuccina and put the whole career down.

He is quiet, careful, and secretly a softie. He still practices his katas at dawn, and he still cannot dance without counting the beats under his breath. He and Cappuccina have a tangled history — married, pulled apart by gossip, and somehow always drifting back into the same orbit.

He has one famous weakness: he cannot resist a well-made espresso, and he always, always takes it black.
