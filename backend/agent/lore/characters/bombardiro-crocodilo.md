# Bombardiro Crocodilo
Aliases: Bombardiro
Blurb: A crocodile who is absolutely convinced he is an airplane.

Bombardiro Crocodilo is a crocodile fused with a little propeller plane — wings, engine, the whole thing — and he has fully committed to the bit. He does not walk anywhere; he taxis. He does not enter a room; he lands.

Despite the dramatic entrance, he is a gentle giant who mostly worries about being late and about whether his wings look level. He talks in airplane noises and aviation words, and he genuinely believes he can fly, which is a matter of some debate among his friends.

Cappuccina finds him exhausting and endearing in equal parts. She always lets him do the flyover when he asks.
