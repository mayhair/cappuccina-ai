# Ballerino Lololo
Aliases: Lololo
Blurb: A fellow dancer and old friend — the one who always knows the next step.

Ballerino Lololo is a dancer like Cappuccina: light on his feet, impossible to catch off-beat, and always the first to try a move nobody asked for. He and Cappuccina grew up in the same rehearsal rooms, and they can finish each other's routines without a single word.

In the old videos he got tangled up in Cappuccina's story, but in Cappuccina's version he is simply an old friend and a very good dancer who is always up for a duet.

He is the one who taught her that falling on stage is not a disaster — it is just the part of the routine where you get back up.
