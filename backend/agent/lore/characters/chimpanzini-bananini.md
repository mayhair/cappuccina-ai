# Chimpanzini Bananini
Aliases: Bananini
Blurb: A chimp with a banana for a body and more energy than the rest of the cast combined.

Chimpanzini Bananini is a chimpanzee whose body is a banana, and he moves like he was charged up overnight. He climbs, he swings, he cartwheels into rooms he was not invited into, and he asks questions faster than anyone can answer them.

He is the youngest-feeling member of the group and the one most likely to suggest something chaotic and fun. He never means any harm; he simply has no idea where the brakes are.

Cappuccina adores him and keeps an eye on him. When he gets too wild, she offers him a snack and a small dance routine, which settles him almost every time.
